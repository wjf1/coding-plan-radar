额度倍率估算方法论

本页公开主站对比表中「额度倍率」的完整估算方法：数据从哪来、折算假设有哪些、哪些口径不能直接互比，以及这套数字不能告诉你什么。对标同类对比站的公开方法论做法——折算口径完全透明，欢迎对照开源项目复核。

This page documents how the quota ratios on the main comparison table are estimated: where the data comes from, which conversion assumptions are applied, which billing semantics cannot be compared directly, and what these numbers cannot tell you. The methodology is fully transparent, mirroring the public-methodology practice of peer sites.

📐 公式与样例 — 倍率是怎么算出来的

Formula and examples

倍率公式Ratio formula

把一个订阅档位的额度——消息条数、credits、Token 量或用量金额上限——按某种单价假设折算成钱，再除以月费，得到「每 1 元月费名义上能买到几元额度」。方法与数值参考开源项目 awesome-coding-plan，本站在对比表中逐条标注来源与采集日期。

A plan's quota (message counts, credits, token allowances or usage-amount caps) is converted into money under a stated unit-price assumption, then divided by the monthly fee — giving the nominal "money-worth of quota per unit of monthly price". The method and figures follow the open-source project awesome-coding-plan; every row on the main table carries its source and retrieval date.

月度额度折算价值 ÷ 月价 = 额度倍率

monthly quota value converted to money ÷ monthly price = quota ratio

汇率换算说明Currency conversion

官方页价格分属美元与人民币两种计价时，按 $1 ≈ ¥7.2 统一量纲后再对比。该汇率仅用于折算量纲，不是汇率承诺，也不改变官方定价本身。

When prices are quoted in both USD and CNY, they are normalized at $1 ≈ ¥7.2 for comparison only. This rate is a dimension-normalization convention, not an exchange-rate promise, and does not alter official pricing.

🧱 三级口径 — 一次估算由三层信息叠加

Three levels of the estimate — each with a different level of confidence

1官方定价与额度数（事实层）Level 1 — Official pricing and quota figures (facts)

价格与额度数取自各平台官方定价页或官方文档，逐条记录来源链接与采集日期（数据文件中的 srcNote 字段，如「claude.com/pricing · 2026-09-13」「bigmodel.cn/glm-coding + docs.bigmodel.cn · 2026-09-13」）。来源分级：官方直采（绿）＞ 实时数据源（青）＞ 聚合参考（蓝）＞ 社区情报（黄，仅作线索，不进入价格表）。官方页有变动时由每日自动巡检挂「待核实」，人工复核后才改表。

Prices and quota figures are taken from each platform's official pricing page or documentation, with the source URL and retrieval date recorded per row (the srcNote field, e.g. "claude.com/pricing · 2026-09-13"). Source tiers: official capture (green) ＞ live data source (cyan) ＞ aggregated reference (blue) ＞ community intelligence (yellow, leads only, never enters the price table). Changes detected by the daily automated patrol are flagged for human verification before the table is updated.

2社区折算假设（估算层）Level 2 — Community conversion assumptions (estimates)

把「非货币额度」折成钱需要单价假设：超量单价（如 Kiro Pro：1000 credits、超量 $0.04/credit）、credits 面值（如 Augment Code INDIE：4 万 credits 价值 $25）、消息条数按官方超购价折算等。这些假设来自 awesome-coding-plan 社区折算表，全部是估算值——官方从不承诺「额度等于多少钱」，且不同平台的假设口径也可能不同。

Converting non-monetary quota into money requires unit-price assumptions: overage unit prices (e.g. Kiro Pro: 1,000 credits, overage $0.04/credit), credit face values (e.g. Augment Code INDIE: 40,000 credits valued at $25), message counts priced at official overage rates, and so on. These assumptions come from the awesome-coding-plan community table and are estimates only — no vendor promises that quota equals a monetary value, and the assumptions can differ across platforms.

3平台差异（口径层）Level 3 — Platform semantics (billing semantics differ)

各家计费口径不同，倍率不能直接互比：Claude 按「5 小时滚动会话 + 每周上限」、Cursor 按模型用量金额、Copilot 按 Premium Requests / AI Credits、国内平台多为积分 / Token 配额。月度倍率最高的 MiniMax（88.65×），其 5h 周期倍率并不突出——倍率高低 ≠ 实际体验好坏，请按自己的使用节奏（5h / 周 / 月）对号入座。

Billing semantics differ across platforms, so ratios are not directly comparable: Claude bills on "5-hour rolling sessions plus weekly caps", Cursor on model-usage dollar amounts, Copilot on Premium Requests / AI Credits, and domestic platforms mostly on points / token quotas. MiniMax has the highest monthly ratio (88.65×) yet an unremarkable 5-hour-window ratio — a higher ratio does not mean a better experience; pick the window (5h / week / month) that matches your own usage rhythm.

🗂️ 数据字段对照 — 口径落在哪个字段

Where these numbers live in the data files

本站全部对比数据由 data/manual/（人工维护）与 data/auto/（每日巡检生成）下的 JSON 驱动（纯静态、零依赖），与本页说明一一对应。核心字段：

All comparison data is driven by JSON files under data/manual/ (human-maintained) and data/auto/ (generated by the daily patrol) — a fully static, zero-dependency setup. Key fields:

  • ratio — 月额度倍率（额度折算价值 ÷ 月价）monthly quota ratio (quota value converted to money ÷ monthly price)
  • periods — 三周期（5h / 周 / 月）额度倍率，对应不同使用节奏three-window (5h / weekly / monthly) quota ratios
  • srcType / srcUrl / srcNote — 来源分级、来源链接与采集日期，逐条可溯源source tier, source URL and retrieval date — every row is traceable
  • rate / rateNote — $1 ≈ ¥7.2 的折算量纲说明the $1 ≈ ¥7.2 dimension-normalization convention
  • pitfalls — 社区反馈汇总（限速、降智、额度争议等），仅供参考community-reported pitfalls (throttling, degraded models, quota disputes), reference only

⚠️ 局限声明 — 这套数字不能告诉你什么

Limitations — what these numbers cannot tell you

  • 倍率 ≠ 实际价值：它是名义折算，不含限速、模型质量、排队与降级等体验差异Ratio ≠ real-world value: it is a nominal conversion and ignores throttling, model quality, queuing and degradation
  • 促销与白嫖未计入：倍率只按标准付费档估算；限时促销在主站「市场动态」、免费额度在「白嫖 / 免费额度」板块单独追踪Promotions and free credits are not included: ratios use standard paid tiers only; time-limited promos and freebies are tracked in their own sections on the main site
  • 覆盖不全：倍率仅覆盖已有社区折算数据的档位；无固定月费的按量计费平台（如 DeepSeek）不适用倍率口径Coverage is incomplete: only tiers with community conversion data are shown; usage-billing platforms without a fixed monthly fee (e.g. DeepSeek) are out of scope for ratios
  • 以官方页为准：AI 订阅价格与额度调整非常频繁，付款前务必以平台官网为准；聚合来源与官方冲突时以官方为准Official pages always win: AI subscription prices and quotas change frequently — always check the vendor's official page before paying; when aggregated sources conflict with official data, official data prevails
